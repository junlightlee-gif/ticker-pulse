Methodology

Each trading day, every US-listed and KRX-listed common stock is tested against the same fixed, rule-based momentum screen — a proprietary combination of technical trend criteria and fundamental growth filters based on the latest regulatory filings (SEC for the US, DART for Korea); the exact rules are not published. Stocks that pass are then ranked by social discussion activity — for the US, bullish-tagged messages on StockTwits in the last 24 hours; for Korea, post counts on each stock's Naver Finance discussion board plus mentions of the company's English name in global financial media RSS headlines. Only aggregate daily counts are collected and shown; no post text or user data is stored.
