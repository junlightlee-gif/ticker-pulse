Methodology

Each trading day, every US-listed common stock is tested against a fixed, rule-based momentum screen: price within 10% of its 52-week high, in an uptrend pullback above its 20-day EMA, minimum liquidity, and growing revenue & earnings per the latest SEC filings. Stocks that pass are then ranked by social discussion activity — the number of bullish-tagged messages on StockTwits in the last 24 hours. Only aggregate daily counts are collected and shown; no post text or user data is stored.
